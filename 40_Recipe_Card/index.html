Tomato Toast

Crisp bread, ripe tomatoes, and a little olive oil.

10 minutes 1 serving Easy

What you’ll need

Tick each ingredient as you go.

Make it

  1. Toast the bread until it is crisp and golden.
  2. Mix the tomato with the olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spoon the tomatoes over the toast.
  4. Tear the basil leaves over the top and serve.
