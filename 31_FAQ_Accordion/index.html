THE STUDY CLUB

A Few Questions

A small weekly meetup for people learning to code.

Do I need experience?

No. Bring your curiosity and start with a small project. Everyone learns at a different pace.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and one thing you would like to practice. A notebook is handy too.

Can I work on my own project?

Yes. You can follow the weekly exercise or work on a project of your own.

How long is a session?

We spend an hour building and leave fifteen minutes at the end to share what we learned.
